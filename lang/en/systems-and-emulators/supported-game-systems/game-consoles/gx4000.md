# Amstrad GX4000

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (493).png" alt="" width="487"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

## Overview

The **GX4000** is Amstrad's only game console, released in Europe in 1990. It is in fact an Amstrad 6128 Plus without keyboard or floppy drive: same Zilog Z80A processor at 4 MHz, 64 KB of memory, and above all the new features of the Plus range, namely a 4096-colour palette, 16 hardware sprites, hardware scrolling and stereo sound.

Games come on cartridge and start as soon as the console is switched on, with no command to type. The console was sold with two two-button controllers and the game Burnin' Rubber.

Launched at the same time as the Mega Drive and facing well-established 8-bit consoles, the GX4000 never found its audience: fewer than thirty games were released and it was withdrawn from sale in 1991. Notable titles include Burnin' Rubber, Pang, Navy Seals, RoboCop 2, Switchblade, Fire & Forget II and Tennis Cup 2.

GX4000 cartridges also work on the 464 Plus and 6128 Plus computers (see the [**Amstrad CPC**](../home-computer/amstrad-cpc.md) page).

## BIOS

No BIOS is needed: the emulators already include the console system.

RetroBat sets the emulated machine itself: a 6128 Plus with cap32 as with Caprice Forever. With Caprice Forever, the **EMULATION > AMSTRAD MODEL** option lets you pick a 464 Plus or a French or Spanish version.

## Controls

### Automatic configuration

RetroBat configures controllers automatically for all emulators (two controllers at most with Caprice Forever).

| RetroBat controller | cap32     | capriceforever |
| ------------------- | --------- | -------------- |
| D-pad / left stick  | D-pad     | D-pad          |
| EAST                | Button 1  | Button 1       |
| SOUTH               | Button 2  | Button 2       |
| WEST                | Combo key | —              |

On the real console, the **Pause** button is on the console itself. It matches the **P** key of the keyboard:

* with cap32, open the virtual keyboard with **WEST + START**, move the cursor to **P** and press **EAST**;
* with a keyboard plugged into the PC, press **P**.

With cap32, the combo key, the virtual keyboard and the other buttons work as on Amstrad CPC (see the [**Amstrad CPC**](../home-computer/amstrad-cpc.md) page).

To configure your controllers yourself, turn off **AUTOCONFIGURE CONTROLLERS**.

### Hotkeys

**cap32** uses the RetroArch hotkeys (see the [Hotkeys](../../../navigation/hotkeys.md) page).

**Caprice Forever**: **HOTKEY + START** to quit.

## Guides

### Playing with a light gun (cap32)

Two GX4000 games are played with a light gun: **The Enforcer** and **Skeet Shoot**.

1. In the game options, turn on **GUNS > ENABLE GUN**.
2. Set **GUNS > LIGHTGUN MODE** to **PHASER**.

The **SHOW CROSSHAIR** option displays a crosshair on screen.

### Disk games

Disk games made for the 464 Plus and 6128 Plus also go in `roms\gx4000`: RetroBat runs them on a 6128 Plus. Automatic start, `.m3u` files and the `#COMMAND:` line work as on Amstrad CPC (see the [**Amstrad CPC**](../home-computer/amstrad-cpc.md) page).
