## Overview

**OutRun** (Sega, 1986) is an arcade racing game: a Ferrari, a passenger and branching roads to the finish line.

RetroBat runs it with the **Cannonball** libretro core, an engine that recreates the arcade game: high resolution, widescreen, 60 frames per second.

The game ROM is not provided: you need the OutRun ROM (revision B) from the MAME romset.
